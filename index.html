How to use this roadmap

Reschedule: drag a bar left/right to a different sprint.

Reassign: drag a bar up/down onto another team's row.

Change duration: drag the right edge of a bar.

Edit details: click a bar to change name, points, colour, team, timing, or delete it.

Workstreams (colours): the 🎨 Workstreams button adds, renames, or recolours categories.

Teams & sprints: ＋ Team adds a row; each team's ⋯ menu renames/recolours/reorders/deletes it. ＋ Sprint adds a column; double-click any sprint, month, or the title to rename.

Sharing (hosted on Firebase): a green “Live” badge means the page is connected to your Firestore database and every edit saves for everyone with the link, live. A grey “Local only” badge means Firebase isn't configured yet (or you opened a local copy) — edits save only in that browser; use Export/Import to move data. See SETUP.md for the one-time setup.

Edit passcode: if one is set, viewers see the roadmap read-only until they click Unlock to edit and enter the code.
